## Overview

This project involved completely reimagining the mobile banking experience for over 2 million users. The existing app had usability issues and wasn't meeting accessibility standards.

## The Challenge

The main challenges were improving task completion rates, reducing customer support calls, and ensuring WCAG 2.1 AA compliance while maintaining brand consistency.

## Process

1. Conducted user research with 50+ participants including users with disabilities
2. Mapped current user journeys and identified pain points
3. Created personas and defined key user scenarios
4. Iterated through multiple design concepts with stakeholder input
5. Prototyped and tested solutions with real users

## Solution

We developed a streamlined interface with improved information architecture, clearer visual hierarchy, and full accessibility support including screen reader optimization and high contrast modes.

## Results

- 40% improvement in task completion rates
- 35% reduction in customer support calls
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance achieved
- 4.7 star rating on app stores (up from 3.2)
